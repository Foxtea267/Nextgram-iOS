## New

- One-Handed Mode (Nagram Settings ▸ Interface ▸ One-Handed Mode): "Chat Search Field at the Bottom", "Search Results from the Bottom" and "Chat Search Results from the Bottom" keep search within reach of your thumb. Each can be turned on separately; all are off by default.
- Open links from the chat list search: paste a t.me link, a tg:// link, or a short form such as "username/123" or "+invitecode", then tap "Open Link" to jump to the chat, message or invite.
- Text Style Toolbar now offers a Persistent Bar below the input field, so you can style selected text with a single tap. Choose Off, Keyboard Panel, Persistent Bar, or Panel + Bar.
- Selection Menu: put your favorite styles (bold, italic, link and more) on the first level of the text selection menu, and choose their order and position (Nagram Settings ▸ Messages ▸ Sending ▸ Selection Menu).
- Chat list: new "Keep New Chats Visible at Top" option, so chats with new messages are not inserted out of view while the list is at the top. Off by default.
- Photo cropping: drag an edge of the crop frame to the edge of the screen and hold to zoom the photo out.

## Improvements

- Nagram Settings are now organized into four tabs: Interface, Chats, Messages and Other. Your existing settings are unchanged.

## Fixes

- Fixed folder unread badges counting pinned chats twice.
- Fixed third-party language packs sometimes reverting to the previous language after a restart, or prompting you to switch back to the suggested language.
- Fixed quotes being split up and losing blank lines when sending without formatting, and collapsed quotes asking for Premium.
- Fixed the expanded profile photo being hard to close on iPad in landscape.
- Fixed extra space under pasted image previews, the crop frame reacting to unrelated drags, long text being cut off in multiline settings fields, and the search filter menu appearing in the wrong place.
- Fixed the icon of the Telegram row in "Open Links in" and an overly long menu item name in Japanese.
- Fixed crashes during heavy list updates, with an invalid external translation endpoint, and with photos of unknown size in the picker, as well as the app occasionally being terminated in the background.
- Fixed read-state syncing looping for some chats and steadily increasing memory use.
